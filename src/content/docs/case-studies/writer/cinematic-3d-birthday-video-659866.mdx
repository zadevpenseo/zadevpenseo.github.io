## 1. Executive Summary

Many commercial and personal 3D animation productions feel rigid because the emotional cadence of the script fails to sync with the background score. This blueprint bridges narrative screenwriting with technical 3D camera directions (Blender / Unreal Engine 5.4), ensuring every audio beat drop aligns with character facial micro-expressions and lighting transitions.
