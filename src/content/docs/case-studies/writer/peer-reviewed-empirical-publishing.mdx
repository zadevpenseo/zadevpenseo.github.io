## 1. The Core Law of Academic Manuscript Engineering

In high-stakes academic publishing, haphazard Markdown-to-Word conversions generate broken files: citations degrade into static text strings rejected by editorial desks of Scopus and SINTA 1-2 indexed journals.

Our manuscript pipeline employs deterministic Python OpenXML generation injecting native Zotero field codes (`ADDIN ZOTERO_ITEM CSL_CITATION`). This allows journal editors and peer reviewers to interact with, inspect, and refresh bibliography trees directly within Microsoft Word.

## 2. PRISMA 2020 Systematic Literature Review (SLR) Protocol

For empirical studies published in IJMME (2024), evidence synthesis followed the rigorous PRISMA 2020 multi-stage funnel:
- **Identification:** Comprehensive queries executed across PubMed, ERIC, OpenAlex, and Google Scholar indexing repositories.
- **Screening:** Automated deduplication and title/abstract filtering against pre-registered inclusion and exclusion criteria.
- **Eligibility:** Full-text methodological quality assessment evaluating quasi-experimental designs and statistical assumption rigor.
- **Inclusion:** Evidence matrix extraction, effect size quantification, and quantitative synthesis.
