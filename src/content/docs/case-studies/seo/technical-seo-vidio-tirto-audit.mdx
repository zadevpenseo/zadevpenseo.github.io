## 1. Executive Summary

High-traffic media and video streaming platforms encounter structural hurdles in organic search visibility:
1. **SERP Snippet Truncation:** Meta titles and descriptions truncated on mobile viewports due to exceeding Google's 600px visual container limits.
2. **GA4 Tracking Gaps:** Misconfigured custom streaming events resulting in missing or duplicated conversion attribution events.
3. **URL Taxonomy & Internal Linking:** Investigative journalism and episodic content orphaned without semantic internal linking to relevant topic clusters.

---

## 2. Audit Methodology & Empirical Findings

### A. Tirto.id: SERP Snippet Optimization & Canonical Taxonomy
- Mapped high-intent journalistic query clusters and restructured title formulas to ensure primary target keywords sit within the first 40 characters.
- Resolved canonical tag discrepancies across dated category archives and multi-page editorial dossiers.

### B. Vidio.com: GA4 Event Tracking & Authority Consolidation
- Audited custom streaming telemetry and User-ID attribution protocols within Google Analytics 4 (GA4).
- Analyzed referring domain quality profiles and architected content-led link acquisition strategies around tentpole sporting broadcasts (World Cup coverage).
