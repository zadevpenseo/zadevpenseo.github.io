The **Data Analysis** pillar is anchored in formal mathematical training (Mathematics Education, 140 Credits, GPA 3.71, Advanced Statistics 89.8 / A-), fused with state-of-the-art computational automation.

We reject speculative modeling and intuition-based approximations. Every statistical model, dataset sanitation pipeline, and automated extraction routine is mathematically verified, computationally bounded, and empirically defensible.

---

## 📊 Analytical Capabilities & Core Disciplines

1. **Mathematical Rigor & Inferential Statistics:** Deterministic classical assumption tests (normality, multicollinearity, homoscedasticity), Structural Equation Modeling (SEM-PLS), and multivariate regressions free from calculation errors.
2. **Psychometrics & Instrument Validation (Rasch & Aiken):** Verifying survey item validity and latent trait measurement using Aiken's V content validity coefficients and Rasch Rating Scale Models.
3. **Resilient CDP & Stealth Scraping Engines:** Real-time data extraction over Chrome DevTools Protocol (CDP) WebSocket sessions capable of navigating dynamic JavaScript single-page applications without rate-limit lockouts.
4. **Arbitrary-Precision Financial Modeling:** Eliminating IEEE 754 binary floating-point round-off errors using integer cents and exact decimal arithmetic (`Decimal`) for corporate tax schedules and dividend audits.
5. **DocuMorph Deterministic Document Pipelines:** Automated extraction of high-density tabular bank statements and balance sheets into clean, structured CSV/Excel relational datasets.
