Single-Source Publishing Architecture for Academic Journal Typesetting

Muhammad Khoiruzzadittaqwa1, Alex Chen2

1 Department of Applied Document Engineering, Jakarta, Indonesia
2 Laboratory of Computational Publishing, Cambridge, UK

Abstract: Multi-format publishing workflows frequently suffer from source drift when Word, XML, HTML, and PDF representations are processed independently. This study proposes a deterministic single-source architecture utilizing JATS XML v1.3 as the canonical foundation. Automated schema validation ensures zero structural errors, while deterministic layout compilers produce press-ready PDF and responsive HTML outputs. Experimental results demonstrate a 94.2% reduction in proof-correction discrepancies and sub-second compilation latencies on low-power hardware.

Keywords: JATS XML, Academic Typesetting, Single-Source Publishing, Typst, Document Engineering

1. Introduction

Academic journals demand stringent compliance across diverse distribution media. While online repositories mandate structured JATS XML for Crossref indexing and PubMed Central ingestion [1], print distributions require high-resolution PDF documents adhering to millimeter-accurate grid layouts [2]. Traditional desktop publishing workflows treat these formats as independent conversion targets, causing late editorial revisions in the PDF to quietly diverge from the archived XML data.

2. Methodology

Our proposed pipeline anchors JATS XML v1.3 as the single source of truth. As shown in Equation (1), the document validation fidelity score V(d) is formulated as the joint probability of schema conformance and cross-format token parity:

V(d) = P(schema_valid) × ∏k=1N (1 − δ(tokenk))      (1)
